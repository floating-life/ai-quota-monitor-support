AI Quota MonitorSupport

Privacy policy

Effective date: October 26, 2026. This policy covers the AI Quota Monitor Android 0.2.0 testing candidate and this support site. The responsible project is AI Quota Monitor, maintained by floating-life. Contact us through the project support channel.

How the app works

AI Quota Monitor displays normalized information supplied by a compatible collector that you configure and control. That information can include account aliases and plan labels, independent model limits, source times, token counts and project aliases. The built-in demo uses fictional data, is clearly labelled, and requires no credentials.

Collector communication

You choose the collector's HTTPS address and provide a dedicated read-only access token. The app sends that token to the chosen collector in an authorization header. Requests may include normalized usage filter identifiers. HTTPS certificate verification is required and redirects are refused. The collector and network infrastructure receive ordinary connection metadata and operate under their own configuration and privacy arrangements.

This candidate has no default developer-hosted data service. We do not receive your collector snapshots or tokens through an app-operated cloud service. The phone does not obtain or refresh your AI provider OAuth credentials, read browser sessions, or send model prompts.

Storage on your device

The collector token uses Android secure storage. Normalized snapshots are stored in app-private local cache, bound to the connection. Display preferences store appearance, number format and statistics timezone. The Android widget receives a display-only snapshot without the collector address or token.

Cached snapshots can remain until you use the app's disconnect-and-clear action or remove the app. Disconnect clears collector credentials and normalized caches; display preferences can remain. The Android candidate disables app backup and data transfer. The app cannot delete your separately controlled collector database, computer logs or provider history.

Services and payments

This candidate includes no advertising, embedded analytics, automatic crash-upload service, hosted app account or enabled purchases. Its widget displays the last snapshot shared by the app and does not independently fetch collector data in the background. Android and Google Play may process platform information under their own terms and your device settings; see Google's privacy policy.

Support and your choices

You decide whether to connect a collector, use the synthetic demo, clear local collector data or remove the app. You control retention and access on your separate collector. If you choose to contact us, we receive the information you submit and use it to investigate and respond to your request.

GitHub support issues are public. Do not post access tokens, passwords, AI credentials or sensitive logs. You may close an issue or ask the maintainer to remove information that we control; GitHub's own retention and privacy terms also apply. When a Play test is active, its private feedback channel is another way to report a problem.

This website

This static site uses no embedded tracking scripts or forms. GitHub Pages hosts the site and can process ordinary web-request metadata under GitHub's privacy statement.

Changes and questions

We will update this page when the app's relevant behavior changes, including before adding hosted services, analytics or purchase features. Questions about this policy can be submitted through the AI Quota Monitor support channel.

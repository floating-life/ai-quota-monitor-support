Testing status

The Android closed-test release is being prepared. Testing access will be shared with invited testers through Google Play. This site does not claim that a store release is already available.

Real data requires a compatible, separately configured Linux/VPS collector and a dedicated read-only token. The app also offers an explicitly labelled synthetic demo without credentials.

Get help

Contact the project maintainer by opening a support issue. Once your Google Play test is active, you can also use its private testing-feedback channel.

Public issues are visible to others. Share the app version, Android version and a description of the problem. Keep access tokens, passwords, AI credentials and sensitive account or project data out of public posts.

What this beta includes

Quota and model windows, known local usage with coverage and timezone controls, display settings, private local caching and an Android widget showing the last in-app snapshot. Purchases and autonomous widget fetching are not enabled in this test.
